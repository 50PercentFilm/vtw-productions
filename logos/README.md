# VTW logo files

The mark is the red drapes on a rail. Wordmark is outlined (no font needed).
Colours: Ink #0A0A0A, Bone #ECE8DF, Curtain red #B3121F, Lit red #D01A2A, Cream #F4EDE0.

- `vtw-mark-*`: the drapes alone
- `vtw-lockup-*`: mark + VTW side by side
- `vtw-stacked-*`: mark above VTW
- `vtw-avatar-*`: square profile pictures (ink and curtain red; `-mark` = drapes only)
- `svg/vtw-og-image.svg`: social share image (PNG is `og-image.png` in the site root)

Variants: `ink` (light backgrounds: black rail and letters, red drapes), `bone` (dark backgrounds: bone rail and letters, lit-red drapes), `curtain` (all curtain red, for bone backgrounds), `cream` (all cream, for red backgrounds).

On the website the drapes change colour with the page (red on black, cream on red, gold on blue) so they always stay lit.
The letters in VTW touch, as on the website. Do not add spacing between them.
